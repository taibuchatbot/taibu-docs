# Your licence and subscription

**Gear icon → Settings → Licence**

Everything about your plan sits on that one page: which plan you are on, how many computers it allows, how many projects you have, your licence key with a copy button, and what happens next.

## The free trial

The trial runs for 7 days and takes a card up front, so it becomes a paid subscription automatically when the trial ends. Taibu shows a bar across the top for the whole trial saying how many days are left and **the exact date your card will be charged**. Cancel any time before that date from your account and nothing is taken.

## When a trial or subscription ends

**The app still opens and everything in it is still yours.** Your projects, files, memory and exports all work exactly as before. You can read them, edit them, and take them with you.

What pauses is the AI: no new conversations, no scheduled routines, no building apps. A bar at the top explains why, with a button to subscribe.

Taibu will never ask you to activate again because a subscription ended. If it asks for a licence key, something else is wrong.

## Cancelling

Cancel from **Manage subscription**, which opens your account on the website. Stripe handles it, so you never have to email anyone.

Cancelling does not cut you off immediately: the AI keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, and Taibu shows that date.

If a payment fails, you get several days of grace before anything stops, because cards fail for ordinary reasons.

## Changing computers or keys

**Change licence key** on the same page lets you paste a different key. Copy your current one first, using the copy button, if you might want to come back to it. Your projects and files are never affected by changing keys.

## Working offline

Taibu checks your licence when it starts and once a day after that. If it cannot reach the licence server it keeps working from what it last knew, for up to a week. After that it needs one successful check before the AI runs again.
