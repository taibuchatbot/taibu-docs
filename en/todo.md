# To-do

Things that need you, in one list. Taibu adds items here when it runs into something only you can decide, and you add your own.

**The tick-list icon in the left rail.** The number on it is how many are still open.

## Adding something

Type it in the box at the top and press Enter. That is the whole thing.

Click any task to rename it. Tick the circle to finish it. Finished work folds away under **Done**, still there if you want to look. The bin appears when you hover, and removing something offers you an undo rather than a confirmation box.

## When Taibu adds something

Agents put work here instead of burying it in a reply you might scroll past. They do it when they hit something they cannot finish themselves:

- a decision only you can make
- a password or API key they must not invent
- something needing your approval, or a call you need to make

Those items carry a violet bar down the left and a chip naming which agent asked, so you can tell at a glance what came from you and what came from Taibu. If the agent left context, it appears under the title.

## What this is not

It is a list, nothing more. Nothing on the page runs anything, sends anything, or starts an agent. Ticking a task off does not tell Taibu to do it, and adding one does not either. If you want work done, ask in a conversation.

## Where it lives

`.taibu/todos.json` in your project, so each project has its own list. It is an ordinary file: you can read it, back it up, or edit it by hand.
